## 🛠️ THE SETTINGS

*Written for Windows 11. Menu names can differ slightly between versions.*

### 1. Turn off ad tracking

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & security → General**.
2. Turn off:
   - **Let apps show me personalized ads by using my advertising ID**
   - **Let websites show me locally relevant content by accessing my language list**
   - **Let Windows improve Start and search results by tracking app launches**
   - **Show me suggested content in the Settings app**

### 2. Limit diagnostic data

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & security → Diagnostics & feedback**.
2. Turn off **Send optional diagnostic data**.
3. Turn off **Tailored experiences**.
4. Set **Feedback frequency** to **Never**.

### 3. Turn off activity history

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & security → Activity history**.
2. Turn off **Store my activity history on this device**.

### 4. Stop tips and suggestions

1. Open **Settings → System → Notifications**.
2. Under **Additional settings**, untick everything listed there. These are the welcome screen, setup suggestions, and tips.

### 5. Clean up the lock screen

1. Open **Settings → Personalization → Lock screen**.
2. Set **Personalize your lock screen** to **Picture** or **Slideshow**.
3. Untick **Get fun facts, tips, tricks, and more on your lock screen**.

### 6. Review app permissions

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & security**.
2. Under **App permissions**, check **Location**, **Camera**, and **Microphone**.
3. Turn off access for any app that doesn't need it.

## ↩️ HOW TO UNDO

- Turn the same switches back on, or re-tick the boxes, in the same places.
