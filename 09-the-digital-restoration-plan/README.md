## 🛠️ BEFORE YOU CHANGE ANYTHING

*Written for Windows 11. Menu names can differ slightly between versions.*

### 1. Turn on System Protection

1. Open **Start**, search for **Create a restore point**, and open it.
2. On the **System Protection** tab, select your system drive (usually **C:**) and click **Configure**.
3. Choose **Turn on system protection**.
4. Set **Max Usage** to around 5–10% and click **OK**.

### 2. Create a restore point

1. In the same **System Protection** window, click **Create**.
2. Give it a name, like `Before Untrash`, and click **Create**.

### 3. Back up your files

1. Open **Start** and search for **Windows Backup**, or copy your files to an external drive.
2. Make sure your **Documents**, **Pictures**, **Desktop**, and anything else important are backed up.
