## 🛠️ THE SETTINGS

*Written for Windows 11. Menu names can differ slightly between versions.*

### 1. Turn off startup apps in Settings

1. Open **Settings → Apps → Startup**.
2. Turn off anything you don't need running as soon as you sign in.
3. Use the **Impact** label as a guide. **High impact** apps slow startup the most.

### 2. Turn off startup apps in Task Manager

1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Esc** to open **Task Manager**.
2. Go to the **Startup apps** page.
3. Right-click an app and choose **Disable**.

### 3. Stop apps reopening after a restart

1. Open **Settings → Accounts → Sign-in options**.
2. Turn off **Automatically save my restartable apps and restart them when I sign back in**.

### 4. Stop Windows finishing setup after updates

1. Open **Settings → Accounts → Sign-in options**.
2. Turn off **Use my sign-in info to automatically finish setting up after an update**.

## ↩️ HOW TO UNDO

- **Startup apps:** turn the app back on in **Settings → Apps → Startup**, or right-click it in Task Manager and choose **Enable**.
- **Restartable apps / finish setup:** turn the switch back on.

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Keep anything from your antivirus, your audio or graphics drivers, or your touchpad enabled.
